## Impact on Workforce Projections

### Female Pelvic Medicine & Reconstructive Surgery (FPMRS)

**Original Assumptions:**
- 2025 Baseline: 1,283 physicians
- 2029 Projected: 1,193 physicians
- Change: **-7.0%** (decline)
- Replacement Ratio: **0.85** (Marginal)
- Annual Fellows: 47
- Annual Retirements: 55.6

**Updated Assumptions (2026):**
- 2025 Baseline: 1,283 physicians
- 2029 Projected: 1,301 physicians
- Change: **+1.4%** (growth)
- Replacement Ratio: **1.08** (Marginal)
- Annual Fellows: 60
- Annual Retirements: 55.6

**Impact:** Increased fellowship positions transformed projected decline into modest growth. Replacement ratio improved from 0.85 to 1.08, now approaching replacement adequacy.

## Clinical and Policy Implications

### FPMRS (Improvement)
**Original:** Projected 90-physician shortage by 2029
**Updated:** Near-replacement level, modest 18-physician growth

**Policy Recommendation:** Recent fellowship expansion appears effective. Maintain 60 positions/year to achieve full replacement adequacy (ratio ≥1.2).

---

### Gynecologic Oncology (Worsening)
**Original:** Projected 103-physician shortage, marginal replacement
**Updated:** Projected 74-physician shortage, insufficient replacement

**Policy Recommendation:** **URGENT.** Reduced fellowship capacity creates critical shortage. Need immediate expansion to 68+ positions/year to achieve replacement adequacy.

**Risk:** Gynecologic Oncology now has the worst replacement crisis among all three subspecialties. With replacement ratio of 0.73, this subspecialty is losing nearly 19 physicians per year net.

---

### MIGS (Slight Decline)
**Original:** Robust growth, 83% surplus capacity
**Updated:** Solid growth, 61% surplus capacity

**Policy Recommendation:** Fellowship reduction prudent given strong replacement ratio. Current 45 positions/year remains adequate for workforce sustainability.

## Replacement Ratio Thresholds

**Classification System:**
- **Adequate:** Ratio ≥ 1.2 (20% surplus capacity)
- **Marginal:** Ratio 0.8-1.19 (near replacement)
- **Insufficient:** Ratio < 0.8 (declining workforce)

**Current Status (Updated Assumptions):**
- ✓ MIGS: 1.61 (Adequate)
- ⚠️ FPMRS: 1.08 (Marginal)
- ❌ Gynecologic Oncology: 0.73 (Insufficient)

---

## Recommendations for Fellowship Policy

### Short-Term (1-2 years)
1. **Gynecologic Oncology:** Expand from 50 to 68 positions/year (+36%)
2. **FPMRS:** Maintain current 60 positions/year
3. **MIGS:** Maintain current 45 positions/year

### Long-Term (5+ years)
1. Monitor retirement acceleration as 1990s certification cohorts reach age 65-70
2. Geographic distribution incentives for rural/underserved areas
3. Part-time practice compensation in replacement calculations
4. International medical graduate pipeline assessment

## Data Sources

**Fellowship Assumptions:**
- Original: 3-year average ACGME graduations (2022-2024)
- Updated: Current ACGME position allocations (2026)

**Retirement Rates:**
- Seven-source hierarchical detection system
- Validated against state medical board records (92.4% sensitivity, 89.7% specificity)
- Annual rates: FPMRS 4.4%, GO 5.2%, MIGS 3.4%

**Projection Method:**
- Monte Carlo simulation (1,000 iterations)
- 4-year projection horizon (2025-2029)
- Linear workforce model: Projected = Baseline - (4 × Retirements) + (4 × Entrants)
